KTIMA DRILLI IN NAXOS ISLAND

Naxos Chora

Naxos Chora, the capital of Naxos Island in the Cyclades, is a charming destination with a blend of historical, cultural, and natural attractions. Here’s what’s good to know:

1. Portara (Temple of Apollo): The most iconic landmark in Naxos Chora
2. Venetian Castle (Kastro), a medieval Venetian castle, dominates the town.
3. Charming Old Town (Old Market): The labyrinthine streets of the Old Town are perfect for wandering.
4. Naxos Chora is close to beautiful beaches, such as Agios Georgios, just a short walk from the town.
5. Cultural and Historical Significance

Golden sandy beaches

Naxos Island is renowned for its stunning beaches, offering something for everyone, from lively, organized spots to quiet, unspoiled coves. Here are the most popular beaches on the island:

Agios Prokopios, Agia Anna, Plaka Beach, Mikri Vigla, Orkos, Alyko, Kastraki, Pyrgaki, Apollonas, Agiassos

Highlights: Its pristine environment and tranquil setting.
Each beach in Naxos offers its unique charm, making the island a paradise for beach lovers. Whether you prefer a bustling atmosphere or serene solitude, you'll find your perfect spot!
